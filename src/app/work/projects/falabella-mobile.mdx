## Overview

At Falabella Financial I worked as a Mobile Developer (Android & iOS) shipping production features inside Banco Falabella's apps across **Peru, Chile, and Colombia** — a regulated financial environment with distributed engineering teams and a high reliability bar.

My focus was the **insurance experience**: bringing it natively into the bank's apps, driving users to it, and securing it — all while keeping the codebase maintainable across multiple apps and countries.

## What I built

- **Reusable insurance feature modules** integrated across multiple Banco Falabella apps and areas — the kind of shared, multi-team code that behaves like an internal SDK. Added the insurance section to **iOS** apps in Chile, Colombia, and Peru, and to **Android** apps in Peru and Colombia.
- **Push-notification and universal/deep-link flows** routing users to specific products (auto, life insurance), **driving a 30% increase in insurance sales**.
- **Soft-token security feature** (Softtoken Seguros) for secure authentication within the insurance flow.
- **Analytics instrumentation** via Google Analytics and Firebase, enabling detailed monitoring of sales performance and data-driven decisions.

## Engineering practices

- Wrote readable, maintainable, performant **Kotlin** (Android) and **Swift** (iOS).
- Owned **CI/CD, automated testing, and crash monitoring**.
- **Led deployments and production launches** across **Google Play, App Store, and Huawei AppGallery**.

## Outcome

Native insurance experiences live across three countries on both platforms, a **30% lift in insurance sales** from the notification/deep-link work, and reliable multi-store release execution — delivered as part of a cross-functional, distributed mobile org.
